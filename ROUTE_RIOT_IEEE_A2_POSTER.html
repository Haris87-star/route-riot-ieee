Abstract & Introduction

Bengaluru’s public transport network serves an immense, constantly shifting commuter demand. While existing transit apps show vehicle GPS coordinates and ETAs, commuters remain completely unaware of in-cabin crowd density and impending congestion spikes before boarding.

Route Riot is an intelligent public-transport assistance framework that combines YOLO-based computer vision for camera-equipped buses with ESP32 + Time-of-Flight (ToF) sensors for non-camera fleets. Occupancy streams are normalized through a high-throughput FastAPI backend to establish live passenger states and forecast upcoming surges.

SENSE ➔ DETECT ➔ PREDICT ➔ REDIRECT
Camera / ToF ➔ Common Event ➔ AI Forecast ➔ Multi-Modal Alternative
Key Novelties:
  • Dual Sensing: Bridges advanced AI cameras with low-cost ToF hardware.
  • Predictive Layer: Anticipates stop-by-stop crowding 15–20 minutes prior.
  • Multi-Modal Sync: Directs passengers to parallel BMTC routes and Namma Metro.

Problem Statement & Scenarios

THE CORE PROBLEM (SLIDE 5):

Bengaluru's bus passengers often do not know the actual crowd level of an approaching bus or how crowded it may become during their journey. A bus being nearby does not necessarily mean it is the best travel choice.

⚠️ Normal Scenario:
  • Passenger wants to travel
  • Bus location is available & ETA is known
  • Passenger boards arriving bus blindly
  • Actual in-cabin crowd level is unknown
  • Result: Severe overcrowding & discomfort
✅ Crowd-Uncertainty / Route Riot:
  • Bus is already highly occupied (>70%)
  • More passengers expected at next stops
  • Nearby alternate bus (500K) has lower crowd
  • Namma Metro option is also available
  • Result: Informed, comfortable journey
Blind Commute: Unpredictable boarding, severe fatigue, missed alternatives.
Route Riot: Pre-boarding clarity, optimal comfort, multi-modal flexibility.
Takeaway: Without predictive occupancy information, passengers miss better available alternatives departing just minutes apart.

Route Riot System Architecture

Hybrid Ingestion: Camera fleets process passenger density via edge YOLO inference; legacy fleets utilize dual doorway ToF break-beam sensors. All streams feed into a unified schema for real-time validation.
